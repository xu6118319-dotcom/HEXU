A structured framework for better procurement decisions.

HEXU's core service is the HEXU Evaluation Framework applied to every manufacturer under evaluation — followed by a structured procurement package that supports confident decisions. This is HEXU's own evaluation approach, not a claimed industry standard.

HEXU Evaluation Framework

Five modules. Every candidate. Every engagement

Before any manufacturer is recommended, it is evaluated against this framework. The output is a documented decision, not a list.

01 · Business Legitimacy

Confirms the manufacturer is a legitimate, transparent legal entity

Legitimacy is the foundation of every other assessment. If the underlying entity cannot be evaluated, no other evidence is meaningful.

  • Corporate identity
  • Registration validation
  • Ownership transparency
02 · Manufacturing Capability

Assesses whether production capability matches the scale of the order

Capability is not the same as equipment list. We look at how production is actually run, and whether it can meet the buyer's specifications at the required volume.

  • Production process
  • Equipment maturity
  • Capacity assessment
03 · Quality Assurance

Reviews how consistently quality is controlled and documented

Quality is judged by what is recorded, not what is promised. We look at inspection workflows, control points, and the documents that actually exist.

  • Quality management
  • Inspection workflow
  • Compliance records
04 · Commercial Reliability

Evaluates how dependable the manufacturer is as a trading partner

Reliability matters as much as capability. We assess how the manufacturer operates commercially — communication, delivery history, and export experience.

  • Export experience
  • Delivery consistency
  • Communication responsiveness
05 · Decision Assessment

Weighs opportunity against risk to form a clear final recommendation

The first four modules produce evidence. The fifth module produces the decision — opportunity, risk, and a single recommendation that the buyer can act on.

  • Opportunity
  • Risk
  • Final recommendation
What You Receive

A structured procurement package, designed to support confident decisions

Every engagement concludes with the same set of deliverables. The output is shareable internally with management teams, procurement leaders, or external stakeholders.

01

Executive Recommendation

A concise executive summary explaining which manufacturer is recommended, why it was selected and the key considerations behind the decision.

02

Supplier Comparison Matrix

A structured comparison of shortlisted manufacturers across capability, quality, commercial reliability, operational risk and strategic fit.

03

Risk Assessment

An independent review of operational, manufacturing and commercial risks together with practical mitigation recommendations.

04

Evidence Package

Supporting documentation including certifications, production information, company evaluation and relevant operational evidence.

05

Decision Rationale

A transparent explanation of how every conclusion was reached so procurement teams can understand the reasoning behind each recommendation.

06

Management-Ready Report

A professionally formatted report that can be shared internally with procurement leaders, management teams or stakeholders.

How HEXU Works

A transparent engagement — paid by the buyer, aligned with the buyer's outcome

HEXU does not publish fixed prices. Engagement scope and fees are defined per project, after the initial requirement discussion.

  1. 01

    Initial requirement discussion

    We discuss the buyer's product, specifications, quantity, timeline and market preference. We confirm whether HEXU is the right fit before any work begins.

  2. 02

    Evaluation scope

    We define what the engagement will cover — which manufacturers, which modules of the HEXU Evaluation Framework, and what the final package will contain.

  3. 03

    Supplier research and assessment

    HEXU carries out the research, discovery and structured evaluation work, drawing on available manufacturing information and documented evidence at each stage.

  4. 04

    Structured procurement insights

    The buyer receives the full structured procurement package: executive recommendation, comparison matrix, risk assessment, evidence and decision rationale.
