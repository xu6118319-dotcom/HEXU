Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information HEXU collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. It applies to this website and the project enquiry form.

Information we collect

When you submit the project enquiry form, HEXU collects the details you provide: your name, email address, industry, product information, quantity, timeline, project requirements and any additional notes. We collect only what is needed to understand and respond to your enquiry.

How we use it

Submissions are reviewed directly by the HEXU team to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to scope a potential engagement. Your information is used only for that purpose. HEXU does not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Retention

Enquiry information is kept only as long as necessary to respond and to maintain a record of the conversation. You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete the personal information you have shared with us. To exercise these rights, contact us through the project enquiry form.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Any technical logs are limited to what is needed to operate the site securely.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project enquiry form. We will respond within one business day.
